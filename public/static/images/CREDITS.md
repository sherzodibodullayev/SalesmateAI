## What is deliberately not here

**No stock portraits.** The founder is represented by a monogram. A stock face
captioned with a real person's name is a misrepresentation regardless of the
licence on the photograph.

**No "business people high-fiving" photography.** Where a card needs to explain
what the software does, it uses a drawn interface mock-up instead — it says
something specific, which a picture of two strangers celebrating does not.
