Introducing SalesmateAI

Most website chat was built to deflect support tickets. Companies point it at sales anyway, so it answers three FAQs, asks for an email, and calls that a lead. Meanwhile the buyer on the other side has already changed how they shop — a majority of B2B software buyers now start vendor research inside an AI chat window rather than a search engine.

SalesmateAI is built for one job: the first sales conversation. It works out what a visitor actually needs, asks the questions your rep would ask, writes the lead record while they speak, and routes it to the person who should call. When it hits the edge of what it knows, it stops and gets a human — which is the part most of this category still gets wrong.

Straight answers

The questions people actually open with.

Short versions here. The agent will take any of them further, and will tell you when it does not know.

Do you have customers yet?

No. SalesmateAI is in pre-launch testing and Tora Labs has no paying customers and no revenue. The founder is validating conversational accuracy, the website embed and the qualification logic before taking money for it. What is open right now is a pilot, run by the founder personally — which is a better deal than being customer four hundred.

How is this different from the chat widget we already have?

Most website chat descends from customer support and was built to deflect tickets. SalesmateAI is built around one job — qualifying and routing a sales conversation — so it asks qualifying questions on its own initiative, writes a structured lead record, and measures itself on qualified leads rather than deflection rate.

What stops it from inventing things about our services?

It answers from a knowledge base you load and control, scoped to your account alone. When a question falls outside it, the designed behaviour is to say so and escalate rather than improvise. That is the single most important design constraint in the product, because a confident wrong answer to a prospect costs more than no answer.

How long does deployment take?

Days. The customer installs a lightweight website component and loads the knowledge sources; the qualification questions and routing rules are configuration, not a build. Enterprise conversational AI platforms typically run weeks-to-months implementation projects — that gap is the whole positioning.

Is voice available?

Not yet. Voice AI is in development and the platform architecture is being built for it, but it is not shipped and the site will not claim it is. Website chat comes first because it is the controlled environment in which to prove the product works.

Will it integrate with our CRM?

CRM and business-system integrations are being built out through the first 24 months. Ask the agent about a specific one and it will tell you honestly whether it is live, planned, or something the founder needs to confirm — rather than nodding along.
