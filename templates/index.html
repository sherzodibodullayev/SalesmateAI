Introducing Hiulix

Most website chat was built to deflect support tickets. MSPs point it at sales anyway, so it answers three FAQs, asks for an email, and calls that a lead. Meanwhile the buyer on the other side has already changed how they shop — a majority of B2B software buyers now start vendor research inside an AI chat window rather than a search engine. The business that just lost a server at 9pm is not filling in a form.

Hiulix is built for one job and one kind of company: the first sales conversation on an MSP’s website. It works out whether the visitor is a 20-seat prospect or a 250-seat one, asks what your account manager would ask, writes the lead record while they type, and routes it to the person who should call. When it hits the edge of what it knows, it stops and gets a human — which is the part most of this category still gets wrong.

An AI sales layer for MSPs

Anything your best account manager does in the first ten minutes, Hiulix does at 3am.

Not a broad platform configured down into a sales bot, and not a horizontal product that happens to tolerate MSPs. One product, one trade: it engages, reads intent, answers from your approved material, qualifies on seats and agreement dates, collects, recommends, schedules, routes and records — and does nothing else.

It reads intent, not keywords.

Every message is classified — informational, exploratory, sales-qualified, pricing, demo, support, or needs-a-human — and the conversation takes the path that matches.

It asks the five questions you would.

Seats or endpoints. Internal IT, or none. Current provider, and when the agreement ends. The compliance driver — SOC 2, HIPAA, CMMC, cyber insurance. And why now. One at a time, inside real answers — never a form dropped on a stranger.

Straight answers

The questions people actually open with.

Short versions here. The agent will take any of them further, and will tell you when it does not know.

Do you have customers yet?

No. Hiulix is in pre-launch testing and Tora Labs has no paying customers and no revenue. The founder is validating conversational accuracy, the website embed and the qualification logic before taking money for it. What is open right now is a pilot, run by the founder personally — which is a better deal than being customer four hundred.

Why only MSPs?

Because the first sales conversation an MSP has is the same conversation every time — seats, internal IT, current provider and agreement date, compliance driver, and why now — and an agent can be genuinely good at a conversation that standardised. A product aimed at every industry has to be configured into usefulness by whoever buys it. Tora Labs' five-year plan names IT service providers among its first markets; this is that line taken seriously rather than hedged.

How is this different from the chat widget we already have?

Most website chat descends from customer support and was built to deflect tickets. Hiulix is built around one job — qualifying and routing an MSP's sales conversation — so it asks the five questions on its own initiative, writes a structured lead record, and measures itself on qualified leads rather than deflection rate.

What stops it from inventing things about our services?

It answers from a knowledge base you load and control, scoped to your account alone. When a question falls outside it, the designed behaviour is to say so and escalate rather than improvise. That is the single most important design constraint in the product, because a confident wrong answer to a prospect costs more than no answer.

How long does deployment take?

Days. The customer installs a lightweight website component and loads the knowledge sources; the qualification questions and routing rules are configuration, not a build. Enterprise conversational AI platforms typically run weeks-to-months implementation projects — that gap is the whole positioning.

Is voice available?

Not yet. Voice AI is in development and the platform architecture is being built for it, but it is not shipped and the site will not claim it is. Website chat comes first because it is the controlled environment in which to prove the product works.

Will it integrate with our PSA?

PSA, RMM and CRM integrations are being built out through the first 24 months. Nothing is live today. Ask the agent about ConnectWise, Autotask or HaloPSA by name and it will tell you that honestly rather than nodding along — which is the behaviour worth testing before you trust it with a prospect.

Can we resell it to our own clients?

Not yet, and there is no white-label or per-client-site program to describe. It is the first thing most MSPs ask, and the reason is obvious from both sides — one MSP reaches dozens of SMBs who each have a website. It is not built and not priced, so the honest answer is that it is a conversation with the founder rather than a product page. The published ranges are per MSP.
