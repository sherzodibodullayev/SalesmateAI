Usage

The second half of the invoice.

Usage is metered separately so the price tracks the cost of running the agent rather than being averaged across everyone. It is also the honest way to sell this: a vendor on a flat fee has an incentive to make your agent answer less.

  • Conversation volume. What the agent actually handled.
  • AI processing. Metered on the model usage behind those conversations.
  • Voice minutes. When voice ships — it has not yet.
  • Additional agents and integrations. Beyond the one your plan includes.

Before you ask

Pricing questions, answered plainly.

Can I pilot it first?

Yes — that is the only thing on offer right now. Early pilots run at reduced or waived cost, and the founder handles onboarding for each one personally. In exchange he wants the honest version of how it went, including the parts that did not work.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

Standard SaaS terms are being finalised alongside the first pilots, and anything a pilot customer signs will be short and readable. If a specific term matters to you, ask the agent and it will pass the question to the founder rather than guess at an answer.

Is it priced per MSP or per client site?

Per MSP. The published ranges cover the agent on your own website, qualifying your own prospects. A per-client-site or white-label arrangement — Hiulix running on the sites of the SMBs you already manage — is the first thing most MSPs ask about, and it is not built and not priced. The agent will tell you that rather than improvise terms; if that is the shape you need, it is a conversation with Nodirbek.

What happens if usage spikes?

Cost unpredictability is a real risk of usage-based pricing and the company treats it as one. Usage is visible in your own analytics from day one, so a spike shows up in the dashboard before it shows up on the invoice. Caps and alerts are part of what the pilot phase is meant to get right.

Why not just build this ourselves?

If you have engineering time to spare, you can assemble a basic website chatbot. The cost that follows is not the build — it is the maintaining, testing and improving, every month, forever, by someone on your payroll. That is the cost the subscription is meant to absorb, and it is the honest comparison rather than sticker price against zero.
